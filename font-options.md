# Typography Options for Rendro Systems

Yeh document **Option 1** aur **Option 2** ke font combinations ki complete details provide karta hai taake aap easily compare kar sakein aur choose kar sakein.

---

## 🅰️ Option 1: Modern Tech & High-Ticket Authority (Recommended)

Ek high-converting SaaS aur digital infrastructure brand ke liye sab se behtareen aur authoritative combination.

### Fonts:
1. **Headings & Main Titles:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`
   - **Google Font:** [Plus Jakarta Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans)
   - **Weights:**
     - Main Hero Title: `900 Black`
     - Section Headings (H2/H3): `700 Bold` ya `800 ExtraBold`
   - **Characteristics:** Crisp geometric sans-serif, tight kerning, modern tech presence.

2. **Body Text, Buttons & UI:** `Inter`
   - **Google Font:** [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)
   - **Weights:**
     - Body Text: `400 Regular` ya `500 Medium`
     - Badges & Navigation: `600 SemiBold`
     - Buttons: `700 Bold`
   - **Characteristics:** World-standard UI font, mobile aur retina screens par highest readability.

### Jahan use hoga:
- **Header:** Navigation links aur "Book a Call" button (`Inter`)
- **Hero Section:**
  - `WEBSITE & MARKETING SYSTEM`: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Black 900)
  - `Built for Contractors`: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Bold 700)
  - Subhead text aur bullet points: `Inter`
  - CTA Buttons: `Inter` (Bold)
- **Features & Comparison Sections:**
  - Section titles aur card titles: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  - Descriptions aur feature lists: `Inter`
- **Footer:** Links, titles aur copyright: `Inter`

## 🅱️ Option 2: Premium Agency & Sleek Geometric Luxury

Slightly rounded, luxurious aur friendly geometric visual aesthetic jo modern high-end agencies use karti hain.

### Fonts:
1. **Headings & Main Titles:** `Outfit`
   - **Google Font:** [Outfit](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit)
   - **Weights:**
     - Main Hero Title: `900 Black`
     - Section Headings (H2/H3): `700 Bold` ya `800 ExtraBold`
   - **Characteristics:** Smooth circular curves ('O', 'C', 'G' perfectly rounded), high-end luxury feel.

2. **Body Text, Buttons & UI:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`
   - **Google Font:** [Plus Jakarta Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans)
   - **Weights:**
     - Body Text: `400 Regular` ya `500 Medium`
     - Badges & Navigation: `600 SemiBold`
     - Buttons: `700 Bold`
   - **Characteristics:** Clean, contemporary geometric sans-serif jo Outfit headings ke sath perfectly blend hoti hai.

### Jahan use hoga:
- **Header:** Navigation links aur "Book a Call" button (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)
- **Hero Section:**
  - `WEBSITE & MARKETING SYSTEM`: `Outfit` (Black 900)
  - `Built for Contractors`: `Outfit` (Bold 700)
  - Subhead text aur bullet points: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  - CTA Buttons: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Bold)
- **Features & Comparison Sections:**
  - Section titles aur card titles: `Outfit`
  - Descriptions aur feature lists: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
- **Footer:** Links, titles aur copyright: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
